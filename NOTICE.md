Any public fork, translation, redistribution or adapted version of this project must show the following notice in a prominent, reasonable location such as its README, documentation footer or credits page:

> Copyright © 2026 **mr-jonam** — licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](LICENSE)

The notice may be adapted for the medium, but it must preserve the project name, repository link, copyright holder, licence reference and an indication of changes. Example: `Modified translation based on the original project; changes by <name>, <date>.`

## What a fork may and may not claim

Forks and derivatives are welcome when they comply with [LICENSE](LICENSE). They may describe their own contributions, but they must not:

- remove or obscure the original attribution;
- present the original work as their own;
- claim or imply approval, sponsorship or affiliation by mr-jonam or the Quality Engineering Learning Hub; or
- apply additional legal or technical restrictions that prevent recipients from using the derivative under CC BY-SA 4.0.

The project name may be used only for factual attribution. It is not a grant to register, market or brand a derivative as the original project.

## Reporting a suspected breach

Contact the repository owner through GitHub, providing the URL, the relevant material and the missing or misleading notice. Do not disclose personal or confidential material in a public issue.
