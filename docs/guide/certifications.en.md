## Paths being phased out
Foundation Level Agile Tester (CTFL-AT) and Agile Technical Tester (CT-ATT) are in sunset: English exams end on 6 May 2027 and non-English exams on 6 November 2027. CTAL-AT v2.0 is the current Agile path. Older CTAL-TA, CTAL-TM, CT-TAE and CT-AI versions are also transitioning or retired.
